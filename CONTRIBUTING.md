# Add a preset

1. Copy [`templates/STYLE.template.md`](templates/STYLE.template.md) to `presets/<your-style>.md`.
2. Fill in the five clauses, two or three modes, and the drift table.
3. Render *a lighthouse on a cliff above the sea* with your example prompt. Add it as `examples/lighthouse/<n>-<your-style>.jpg` (512px).
4. Add a row to [`presets/README.md`](presets/README.md). Open a PR.

House rules: exact hexes, a ban list, one example prompt, no real brands or living artists' names.
