# Style contract: Bauhaus

> Every image looks like a 1920s Bauhaus poster: circles, squares and triangles in primary colours on off-white, built with a ruler and a compass.

## Contract (every image, every mode)

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Finish** | Flat matte lithograph colour, crisp geometric edges, faint paper grain and slight ink unevenness over the whole frame. No gradients, no shading, no photographic detail. |
| **Palette** | Anchor: Bauhaus red `#D62828`. Supporting: yellow `#F2C230`, blue `#1D4E89`, black `#111111`. Neutral: off-white paper `#EFE9DC`. Never: pastels, gradients, green, purple, neon. |
| **Composition** | The subject built only from circles, rectangles, triangles and thick straight lines, arranged on a strict grid with strong diagonals. Lots of flat paper. Copy zone: one clean rectangle of paper. |
| **Subject** | One subject, abstracted into geometry until it is just recognisable. No people with faces, no lettering, no logos. |
| **Mood** | Rational, bold, optimistic, playful. Never: organic, soft, nostalgic-sepia, busy. |

## Modes (pick exactly one per image)

| Mode | Clause to copy into the prompt |
| --- | --- |
| **Geometric poster** | a 1920s Bauhaus lithograph poster built from circles, rectangles and triangles on a strict grid |
| **Photomontage** | a 1920s constructivist photomontage, black-and-white photo cutouts combined with flat primary-colour shapes |
| **Paper construction** | a Bauhaus paper construction exercise, cut and folded coloured card photographed flat with soft shadows |

## Off-brand tells

| Drift | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Shading or 3D rendering | Say "completely flat colour, no shading, no gradients." |
| Organic curves | Say "built only from perfect circles, straight lines and triangles." |
| Fake German lettering | Put "no text, no letters" on the last line. |

## Example prompt

> A lighthouse on a cliff above the sea at night. Rendered as a 1920s Bauhaus lithograph poster built from circles, rectangles and triangles on a strict grid: the tower a tall black rectangle, the light a perfect yellow #F2C230 circle, its beam a long red #D62828 triangle, the sea a band of blue #1D4E89. Off-white paper #EFE9DC with lots of flat empty space. Flat matte colour, crisp edges, faint paper grain over the whole frame, no shading, no gradients. Bold, rational, playful. Full bleed, no border. The red is #D62828. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks.
