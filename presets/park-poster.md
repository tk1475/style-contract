# Style contract: Park Poster

> Every image looks like a mid-century screen-printed national park poster: flat layered inks, a big landscape, long shadows, total calm.

## Contract (every image, every mode)

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Finish** | Screen print: flat opaque ink layers with crisp edges, no gradients except stepped bands of colour, subtle paper texture and faint ink wear over the whole frame. |
| **Palette** | Anchor: deep pine green `#1F4D3A`. Supporting: sunset orange `#E87A3C`, dusk sky blue `#7FB2C9`, sand `#E9D8A6`. Accent: warm red `#C0392B`, once, small (a tent, a jacket, a lit window). Neutrals: cream `#F4EBD9`, near-black `#1B1B1B`. Never: neon, pastel pink, purple, glossy highlights. |
| **Composition** | Layered depth in four to six flat planes (foreground, mid, far ridges, sky). Low horizon or towering landform. Copy zone: a clean sky band across the top third when text will be overlaid. |
| **Subject** | One landscape feature as the hero (a peak, a canyon, a lake, one tree). People only as tiny figures for scale. No crowds, no vehicles, no modern buildings, no lettering inside the image. |
| **Mood** | Calm, timeless, adventurous, warm. Never: dramatic HDR, gloomy, busy, digital. |

## Modes (pick exactly one per image)

| Mode | Clause to copy into the prompt |
| --- | --- |
| **Screen-print poster** | a 1950s screen-printed travel poster with six flat ink layers and crisp stepped colour bands |
| **Gouache painting** | a mid-century gouache landscape painting, matte opaque brushwork, simplified shapes |
| **Woodcut** | a hand-carved woodcut print, visible gouge marks in the sky, three inks |

## Off-brand tells

| Drift | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Photoreal or HDR landscape | Restate the mode clause; add "flat ink layers, no photographic detail." |
| Smooth sky gradient | Say "the sky is stepped bands of flat colour." |
| Red used everywhere | The red is an accent: name the one small object it appears on. |
| Too many details | Cut to one hero feature and four to six planes. |

## Example prompt

> A single towering granite peak rising above a still alpine lake, with one tiny figure in a warm red jacket standing on the shore for scale. Rendered as a 1950s screen-printed travel poster with six flat ink layers and crisp stepped colour bands. Deep pine green #1F4D3A forests in the foreground, dusk sky blue #7FB2C9 and sunset orange #E87A3C in stepped bands across the sky, sand #E9D8A6 light on the rock, the red #C0392B appearing only on the jacket. Flat opaque inks with crisp edges, subtle paper texture and faint ink wear over the whole frame. Calm, timeless, adventurous; the top third is a clean band of sky. Full bleed, no border. The forest green is #1F4D3A. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks.
