# Style contract: Riso Zine

> Every image looks like a page from a risograph-printed zine: two or three fluorescent inks on cream paper, slightly misregistered, bold and hand-made.

## Contract (every image, every mode)

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Finish** | Risograph print texture over the whole frame: visible ink grain, soft dot screen in the tones, slight misregistration between ink layers, flat ink with no gradients beyond the dot screen, on uncoated cream paper. |
| **Palette** | Anchor: fluorescent pink `#FF48B0`. Supporting: teal `#00838A`, yellow `#FFE800`. Where two inks overlap, they mix into a darker overprint colour. Neutrals: cream paper `#F5F0E6`, no true black. Never: photographic full colour, neon gradients, purple glow, grey. |
| **Composition** | One bold graphic shape or figure, large, often cropped by the edge. Plenty of bare paper. Copy zone: one clean band of paper or a flat ink field when text will be overlaid. |
| **Subject** | One subject: an object, an animal, a hand, a plant, a person. Treated graphically, never photoreal. No logos, no real brand marks, no lettering in the image. |
| **Mood** | Playful, warm, DIY, confident. Never: corporate, slick, glossy, futuristic. |

## Modes (pick exactly one per image)

| Mode | Clause to copy into the prompt |
| --- | --- |
| **Riso illustration** | a two-colour risograph illustration with bold simple shapes and hand-drawn edges, like an indie zine cover |
| **Riso photo** | a black-and-white photograph reprinted as a two-colour risograph halftone, coarse dot screen, each ink slightly offset |
| **Cut-paper collage** | a cut-paper collage reprinted on a risograph, torn edges, overlapping flat ink shapes |

## Off-brand tells

| Drift | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Smooth digital gradients, glossy finish | Escalate: "coarse risograph ink grain, visible dot screen, misregistered layers, uncoated paper." |
| Full-colour photo look | Restate the mode clause; say "only two inks, fluorescent pink #FF48B0 and teal #00838A." |
| Pure white background | Say "cream uncoated paper #F5F0E6, never white." |
| Perfect alignment | Add "the pink layer is offset slightly from the teal layer." |
| Gibberish lettering | Move "no text, no letters" to the last line. |

## Example prompt

> A single large monstera leaf cropped by the right edge of the frame, with a small yellow snail climbing its stem. Rendered as a two-colour risograph illustration with bold simple shapes and hand-drawn edges, like an indie zine cover. Fluorescent pink #FF48B0 and teal #00838A on cream uncoated paper #F5F0E6, with one small touch of yellow #FFE800 on the snail; where pink and teal overlap they print a dark overprint. Coarse risograph ink grain over the whole frame, visible dot screen in the tones, the pink layer slightly misregistered from the teal. Playful, warm, hand-made; one bold shape with lots of bare paper. Full bleed, no border. The pink is #FF48B0. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks.
