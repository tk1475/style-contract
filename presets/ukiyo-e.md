# Style contract: Ukiyo-e

> Every image looks like an Edo-period Japanese woodblock print: indigo, bold outlines, flat colour with soft bokashi fades, and a sea that wants to eat you.

## Contract (every image, every mode)

| Part | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Finish** | Traditional woodblock print: visible wood grain in flat colour areas, bold black key-block outlines, soft bokashi gradient only in the sky and water, aged washi paper texture over the whole frame. |
| **Palette** | Anchor: Prussian blue `#1F3A60`. Supporting: pale indigo `#7A9CB8`, washi cream `#EDE3CC`, muted rust `#B5523B`. Accent: vermilion `#D9432B`, once, small. Never: neon, purple, pure white, photographic colour. |
| **Composition** | One dramatic natural form (a wave, a mountain, a tree) in the foreground or middle, a tiny human element for scale, strong diagonals, a flat sky band with a bokashi fade. |
| **Subject** | Nature first, people tiny. No modern objects, no text cartouches, no seals or signatures. |
| **Mood** | Dramatic, timeless, contemplative. Never: cute, anime, glossy, modern. |

## Modes (pick exactly one per image)

| Mode | Clause to copy into the prompt |
| --- | --- |
| **Landscape print** | an Edo-period ukiyo-e woodblock landscape print in the style of Hokusai and Hiroshige |
| **Night print** | a Shin-hanga woodblock night print, deep blues, soft moonlight bokashi, glowing lanterns |
| **Kacho-e** | a ukiyo-e bird-and-flower woodblock print, one bird and one branch on a plain ground |

## Off-brand tells

| Drift | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Anime or digital painting look | Say "carved woodblock print, black key-block outlines, wood grain visible in flat colour." |
| Fake seals and kanji | Say "no seals, no signatures, no writing." |
| Smooth airbrushed sky | Say "flat colour with a single soft bokashi fade at the top." |

## Example prompt

> A lone lighthouse on a rocky cliff while a huge curling wave breaks below it, a tiny figure on the path. Rendered as an Edo-period ukiyo-e woodblock landscape print in the style of Hokusai and Hiroshige. Prussian blue #1F3A60 and pale indigo #7A9CB8 water, washi cream #EDE3CC foam and sky, muted rust #B5523B rocks, a single small vermilion #D9432B glow in the lamp. Bold black key-block outlines, visible wood grain in the flat colour, a soft bokashi fade at the top of the sky, aged washi paper texture over the whole frame. Dramatic, timeless. Full bleed, no border. The blue is #1F3A60. No text, no seals, no signatures, no watermarks.
